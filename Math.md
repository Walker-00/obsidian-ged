
## Topics Covered in GED Math
1. **Quantitative Problem Solving**
   - Number operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)
   - Ratios, proportions, and percentages
   - Fractions and decimals
   - Exponents and roots

2. **Algebraic Problem Solving**
   - Expressions, equations, and inequalities
   - Linear equations and functions
   - Quadratic equations
   - Systems of equations

3. **Geometry**
   - Properties of shapes (triangles, circles, polygons)
   - Perimeter, area, and volume
   - The Pythagorean theorem
   - Coordinate geometry

4. **Data Analysis and Probability**
   - Graphs and tables
   - Statistical measures (mean, median, mode)
   - Probability concepts
   - Word problems involving data

## Formulas to Remember
### Basic Formulas
- **Area of a rectangle**: A = l \times w
- **Area of a triangle**: A = \frac{1}{2} b \times h
- **Area of a circle**: A = \pi r^2
- **Circumference of a circle**: C = 2\pi r
- **Volume of a rectangular prism**: V = l \times w \times h

### Algebraic Formulas
- **Slope of a line**: m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}
- **Quadratic formula**: x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}

### Pythagorean Theorem
- a^2 + b^2 = c^2 (right triangle)

### Probability
- P(A) = \frac{\text{Number of favorable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of outcomes}}

## Practical Questions
### Quantitative Problem Solving
1. Simplify 3/4 + 2/5.
2. If 60% of a number is 72, what is the number?
3. Calculate 2^3 \times 5.

### Algebraic Problem Solving
1. Solve for x: 3x + 5 = 20.
2. Simplify (2x + 3) - (x - 4).
3. Find the roots of x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0.

### Geometry
1. Find the area of a circle with a radius of 7 cm.
2. Calculate the volume of a rectangular box with dimensions 5 \, \text{cm} \times 4 \, \text{cm} \times 3 \, \text{cm}.
3. Use the Pythagorean theorem to find the hypotenuse of a triangle with legs of 6 cm and 8 cm.

### Data Analysis and Probability
1. What is the mean of the numbers 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25?
2. If a dice is rolled once, what is the probability of getting an even number?
3. Interpret a bar graph showing monthly expenses.

## Study Tips
1. Practice with GED sample tests and questions.
2. Memorize key formulas listed above.
3. Use a calculator effectively (only allowed for certain sections).
4. Focus on understanding word problems, as they are common on the test.
5. Review your weaknesses in algebra, geometry, or probability.
